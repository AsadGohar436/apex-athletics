Zero. We do not believe in surprise setup fees, annual facility maintenance charges, or lock-in penalties. You pay the exact advertised tier price.

Can I pause or freeze my membership if I travel?

Yes. Pro and Titan members can freeze their membership directly inside the Apex Mobile App for up to 60 calendar days per year at zero penalty.

Can I test the facility before committing?

Absolutely. Every prospective athlete is eligible for our complimentary 7-Day All-Access VIP Pass with full access to both strength floors and recovery amenities.
